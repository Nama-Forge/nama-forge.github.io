Privacy Policy

Applies to Sort Foundry. Last updated: 12 November 2026.

Summary

Sort Foundry has no accounts and asks for no name, email address, contacts, photos or location. Your progress is stored on your device. The game shows ads through Google AdMob, and AdMob collects some device data to do that. This page explains what that data is and what choices you have.

Data stored on your device

Your level, coins, settings, daily bonus streak and purchase status are saved in a file on your device. Nama Forge does not receive this file. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear the app's data.

Advertising

The game uses Google AdMob to show ads. To deliver ads, limit how often you see the same one, and detect fraud, AdMob may collect:

  • the advertising ID of your device;
  • your IP address, which shows your approximate location;
  • device model, operating system version, language and network type;
  • which ads were shown and whether you interacted with them;
  • diagnostic data such as crash information from the ad component.

Google processes this data under its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices about ads

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before showing personalised ads. You can change your answer at any time in the game's settings.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • The one-time "Remove ads" purchase turns off ads that appear between levels. Ads you choose to watch for a reward stay available.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Google Play. Nama Forge does not see or store your payment details. The game receives only a confirmation that a purchase was made.

Children

Sort Foundry is not directed at children under 13, and Nama Forge does not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Retention and deletion

Nama Forge keeps no personal data on its own servers, so there is nothing for it to delete. To remove the data on your device, uninstall the game. For data held by Google, use the controls in your Google account and the device settings above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer contact address listed on the Sort Foundry page on Google Play.

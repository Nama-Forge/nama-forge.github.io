Sort Foundry

In development

A colour-sort puzzle. Tap a silo, tap another, and stack the ingots until every silo holds a single colour.

  • Endless levels that are always solvable
  • Mystery ingots that reveal themselves as you dig
  • Shapes on every colour, so it plays without colour vision
  • Undo, hints and an extra silo when you get stuck

Android first, iOS to follow
Status
Playable build, testing on devices

About

MVP Games is a one-person independent studio. The name comes from the founder's initials and from the way the games are made: build the smallest version that is fun, put it in players' hands, and improve it from there.
